Download the app
  1. Open this page on an Android phone and tap Download the app.
  2. Tap Open when the download is done.
  3. The first time, Android asks to allow installs from your browser. Tap Settings, turn it on, and go back.
  4. Tap Install. If Play Protect warns you, tap Install anyway.
