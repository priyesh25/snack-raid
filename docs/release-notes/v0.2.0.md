The first Android app.

- Each player on a phone can have their own raider, with a name and their own progress. Up to 4.
- Save codes move a raider to another phone.
- The app tells you when a new version is ready.
- The Back button pauses a night, or goes back one menu.

Install: download snack-raid.apk on an Android phone and open it. The first time, Android asks you to allow installs from your browser. Updates install over the top and keep your progress.
