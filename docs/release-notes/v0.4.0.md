Real sounds, night music, and easier steering.

- Real recorded sounds: Biscuit's woof, Dad's snore and his snort when he wakes up, Mom's cough, Baby Boo crying, creaky boards, squeaky doors, footsteps, the toilet flush, thunder, the alarm clock and more.
- Night ambience: crickets outside and the clock ticking. It goes quiet when someone is awake.
- New night music: a slow, soft tune while everyone sleeps. It turns into a fast sneaky chase when someone wakes up.
- Grown-ups mumble in low, sleepy voices instead of squeaky ones.
- Steer from anywhere on the screen, not just the left half.
- Tap a spot and the raider walks there. Tap twice to run there. Drag at any time to take over.
- A tiny buzz when you go from tiptoe to walk to run, so you can feel your speed.
