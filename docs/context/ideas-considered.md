# Ideas that were considered

Seven ideas were pitched. All share one shape: a simple premise, funny chaos, and a strong pull to try one more time.

| Idea | The pitch | Why it was or was not picked |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Egg Heist | Sneak into a farm and steal eggs. Every egg makes you slower and louder. Chickens peck, geese chase | Priyesh's own seed idea. Its greed rule (more loot, more risk) was carried into Snack Raid |
| Junkyard Mechs | Build a battle robot from household junk. Each part changes how it fights | Closest to Mech Arena. Bigger build, less unique |
| Be the Boss | You are the final boss. Design your attacks, then tiny heroes come at you | Fun flip, but hard to make fair on a phone |
| Floor is Lava | The house floods with lava. Jump across furniture that sinks and tips | Simple, but many games already do it |
| **Midnight Snack Raid** | Sneak from bed to fridge without waking parents, the dog or the baby | **Picked.** Every kid has lived it, the humor writes itself, and stealth suits short levels |
| Slime Lab | Mix two slimes to make a creature with both powers | Good collecting hook, weaker story |
| Pet Kaiju | Hatch a tiny monster. What you feed it decides what it becomes | Charming, but more of a pet game than a level game |

## What Snack Raid borrowed from the others

- **From Egg Heist:** greed is the fun. Hands hold 2 snacks, and every extra trip is a risk.
- **From Junkyard Mechs:** gadgets you collect and level up. One Sugar Dream is a stomping Snack Mech level.
- **From Be the Boss:** the family are puzzles, not villains. In the last boss, the whole family turns out to be raiders too.
