# The brief

What Priyesh asked for, in the order he asked. November 2026.

## The ask

- Design a game, then design its UI, then build the app.
- It runs on Android: native, or hybrid (an HTML app inside an Android wrapper).
- It should appeal to 10 to 12 year olds. Someone who likes Roblox, Mech Arena and Nintendo.
- It should be fun, very entertaining and engaging.
- "Think of something unique." His own seed idea was a "steal an egg" game where chickens attack when you steal.

## How it narrowed down

1. Seven game ideas were pitched (see `ideas-considered.md`).
2. He picked **Snack Raid** (pitched as Midnight Snack Raid).
3. He asked for the full rules "with all bells and whistles": levels, coins, prizes, surprises, special tools for handling situations and sneaking. Graphics included. No building yet.
4. He then asked for everything to go into one project folder in the right sub-folders: context, game details, thinking, memory, rules, graphics. And for the app to be built into the same folder, ready to commit, push and host.
5. He confirmed every open decision as proposed.
6. He asked for 10 to 20 promo codes for benefits and features, and 10 cheat codes that make the game easier.
7. He asked for a playable build with no more questions. Assumptions were to be made and written down.

## Constraints

- Android first. Hostable as a website.
- Kid-safe by design.
- The folder must be ready to commit and push. Nothing was committed for him.

## Who it is for

- A 10 to 12 year old player.
- A parent who should be happy to hand the phone over.
